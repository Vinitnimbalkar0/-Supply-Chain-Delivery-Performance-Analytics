# Recommendations

## REC-01 — Investigate First Class Fulfillment

### Evidence

First Class has an observed late-delivery rate of approximately **97.4%**.

### Action

Conduct a detailed review of:

- Warehouse processing
- Shipping commitments
- Order handoffs
- Product mix
- Order size
- Market/region mix
- Transportation processes

### Priority

**High**

### Expected Outcome

Identify the operational stages associated with the observed First Class performance gap.

---

## REC-02 — Prioritize Using Risk × Business Impact

Do not prioritize only by late rate.

Use:

```text
Delivery Risk
+
Order Volume
+
Sales Exposure
+
Profitability
```

### Priority Matrix

| Condition | Priority |
|---|---|
| High late rate + high sales exposure | Critical |
| High late rate + low sales exposure | Investigate |
| Low late rate + high sales exposure | Protect |
| Low late rate + low sales exposure | Monitor |

---

## REC-03 — Investigate High-Value Product Categories

Identify categories combining:

- High sales
- High order volume
- Elevated late rate
- High shipping variance
- Weak profitability

### Expected Outcome

Determine whether product mix or category-specific fulfillment requirements are associated with delivery risk.

---

## REC-04 — Monitor High-Demand Periods

Create a recurring review of:

- Monthly orders
- Sales
- Late rate
- Shipping variance
- Shipping-mode mix

Focus on periods where:

```text
Order Volume ↑
+
Late Rate ↑
```

This is a hypothesis for operational pressure, not proof of causation.

---

## REC-05 — Evaluate Discount Effectiveness

Compare discount buckets against:

- Sales
- Profit
- Margin
- Order volume
- Late rate

### Business Question

> Does the additional sales volume generated by higher discounts justify the reduction in profitability?

---

## REC-06 — Prioritize High-Revenue Markets Carefully

Europe and LATAM represent the largest sales markets in the current analysis.

When delivery risk is elevated, high-revenue markets deserve attention because improvements may affect a larger revenue base.

However, market size should always be evaluated together with risk and profitability.

---

## REC-07 — Improve Operational Data Collection

For stronger root-cause analysis, collect:

- Carrier
- Warehouse
- Fulfillment center
- Picking time
- Packing time
- Handover time
- Transportation time
- Delivery attempts
- Logistics cost
- Refunds
- Compensation
- Customer complaints
- Cancellation reasons

---

## REC-08 — Do Not Call Late Sales "Lost Revenue"

Use:

> **Sales associated with late-delivery orders**

instead of:

> Lost revenue caused by late delivery

unless supporting data exists.

Actual financial loss should be calculated using evidence such as refunds, compensation, incremental logistics cost, cancellations, churn or lost future purchases.

---

## Recommended Management Sequence

```text
1. Investigate First Class performance
             ↓
2. Identify high-revenue + high-risk segments
             ↓
3. Investigate high-value product/category risks
             ↓
4. Analyze high-demand periods
             ↓
5. Evaluate discount vs profitability
             ↓
6. Collect additional operational data
```

## Final Recommendation

> **Management should prioritize operational investigations where high delivery risk intersects with significant revenue exposure and weak profitability. First Class is the strongest observed delivery-risk signal and should be investigated across fulfillment, product, geographic and order characteristics before changing the shipping strategy.**
